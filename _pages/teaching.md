Wintersemester: Seminar **Einführung in Eyetracking**. The course introduces the foundations of eye-tracking methodology, covers study design and data collection, and provides practical guidance on preprocessing, visualization, and basic analysis.

Participants work with real datasets, learn about common pitfalls and data quality checks, and discuss ethical considerations in research with human participants.
