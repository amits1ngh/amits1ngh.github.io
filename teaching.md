<section class="section">
  <h1>Teaching</h1>
  <p>
    Wintersemester: Seminar <strong>Einf&#xFC;hrung in Eyetracking</strong>.
    The course introduces the foundations of eye-tracking methodology, covers study design and data
    collection, and provides practical guidance on preprocessing, visualization, and basic analysis.
  </p>
  <p>
    Participants work with real datasets, learn about common pitfalls and data quality checks, and
    discuss ethical considerations in research with human participants.
  </p>
</section>
