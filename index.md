<section class="section">
  <h2>Approach</h2>
  <p>
    I investigate questions such as how individuals engaged in conversation leverage a myriad of
    visual and verbal cues to establish shared context, negotiate mutual understanding, and
    coordinate joint actions.
  </p>
  <p>
    I mostly use eye tracking (mobile and remote), HRI experiments, and statistical modeling to
    explore these questions.
  </p>
  <p>
    My hope is to use these findings to incorporate verbal scaffolding strategies in artificial
    systems that are tailored to users' capabilities, informed by their interaction history, and
    grounded in cognition.
  </p>
</section>
